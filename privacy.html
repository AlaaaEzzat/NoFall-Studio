Privacy Policy

NoFall Studio games · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NoFall Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares information when you play our mobile games, including BoneBreaker (each a "Game"). By installing or playing a Game you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

Our games are made for different audiences. BoneBreaker is intended for players aged 13 and over. Section 10 explains how we handle games that may be played by younger children.

1. Who we are

NoFall Studio is an independent, online game studio. We work remotely and are not tied to a single country, and our location may change over time. We develop and publish the Games. You can contact us at any time at nofallstudio@hotmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The information below is collected automatically by third-party services built into the Game. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device only.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Game.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To show advertising, including ads that may be personalized, and to measure how well ads perform.
  • To process in-app purchases and restore items you already bought.
  • To respond to your messages and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where the law requires us to have a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Consent, for personalized advertising and the use of your advertising ID for ads where consent is required. You can withdraw it at any time (see sections 6 and 11).
  • Legitimate interests, for crash reporting, security and improving the Game, where these interests are not outweighed by your rights.
  • Performance of a contract, to deliver in-app purchases you ask for.
  • Legal obligation, where we must keep or share information to comply with the law.

5. Third-party services

The Game uses services provided by other companies. They may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing (installation, updates, payments and crash and performance reports): Google Privacy Policy.

These providers receive the information in section 2 that is relevant to their service. They may act as independent controllers of that information. We do not give them your name or contact details, because we do not have them.

In summary, the types of parties that receive information from the Game are: (1) advertising services (Google AdMob and the advertisers and ad partners it works with); (2) the app store and payment provider (Google Play), which also provides crash and performance reports; and (3) public authorities or other parties, only where the law requires it or to protect our rights, or if the studio is sold or merged (in which case the new owner must honor this policy). We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and your choices

Ads in the Game are provided by Google AdMob. You can control ad personalization at any time on your Android device: open Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of ad personalization or to reset or delete your advertising ID. If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the Game's settings.

7. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so that we can unlock the item.

8. Data retention and deletion

We do not run player accounts, and we do not collect or store personal information on our own servers. The information described in section 2 is collected and held by Google (through AdMob and Google Play) for the periods set in Google's own policies. The only information we see ourselves is aggregated reports and crash reports from Google Play Console, which we keep only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy.

Because Google holds the data, you can delete or reset your advertising ID and manage ad data in your device and Google account settings. To ask us to delete any data we hold about you, email nofallstudio@hotmail.com with the name of the Game. If you can, include your device's advertising ID so we can help you use Google's own deletion and ad-settings tools. We will respond within the time required by law (usually within 30 days). We may keep limited information where we must do so to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent fraud. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings or uninstall the Game at any time.

9. Data security

Data sent by the Game to its advertising and payment providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We limit the information we hold to what is needed and take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

10. Children's privacy

We make games for different age groups, and each Game's store listing shows who it is intended for. BoneBreaker is intended for players aged 13 and over (or the minimum age for consent in your country, if it is higher) and is not directed to children under that age.

For any Game that is made for or may appeal to children under 13 (or under the age set by local law), we follow the rules that apply to children's apps, including Google Play's Families Policy and laws such as COPPA, the GDPR and other national data protection laws. For those Games we will:

  • not show personalized or interest-based advertising, and only use ad services and settings that are approved for children;
  • collect only the minimum data needed to run the Game, and not collect personal information such as a name, email address or precise location;
  • not let children make purchases or follow links outside the Game without a parent's involvement.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that breaks these rules. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us information, contact nofallstudio@hotmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or move the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold account-level data about you, most information is tied to your device's advertising ID. To make a request, email nofallstudio@hotmail.com. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your local data protection authority (for example your national supervisory authority in the EU and UK, or the relevant authority in your country; see also sections 12 and 13).

12. Additional information for US residents

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Personalized ads shown through Google AdMob may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. You can opt out by turning off ad personalization in your Android settings (see section 6) or by emailing us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without the permission the law requires.

13. Users in other countries

Data protection laws differ around the world, and some countries (for example Egypt, Brazil, India, Canada and South Korea) give you rights similar to those described in section 11, such as access, correction, objection and withdrawal of consent. We respect the rights that apply to you under the law of the country where you live. We ask for your consent to personalized advertising separately from this policy, and it is never a condition of playing the Game. To use your rights, email nofallstudio@hotmail.com. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

14. International transfers

Our service providers (such as Google) process information on servers in countries other than your own, including countries that may have different data protection laws. Where the law requires it, we rely on your consent, approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, or another lawful basis for these transfers.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and where a change is significant we will tell you in the Game or on this page. Please review this page periodically.

16. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy? Email nofallstudio@hotmail.com.
